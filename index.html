How Swiftdropp works

Three steps from booking to doorstep. The customer, driver and operations views below all run on the same live order data in this demo.

STEP 01

Book & get a quote

Enter pickup and drop-off, pick a parcel size and timing — the price and ETA appear instantly, no account needed to check.

STEP 02

A driver is assigned

The nearest available driver accepts the job. You see who they are, what they drive and exactly where they are.

STEP 03

Track to the door

Follow each milestone — collected, in transit, delivered — with proof of delivery captured at the doorstep.

One upfront price per vehicle — no surprises

Pay only for the space you need. Every price below is the final price: no fuel, early or late surcharges, ever. Multi-drop routes and scheduled pickups are priced before you book.
